APP CARD - Instagram (com.instagram.android). What this project learned the hard way, on IG 446:

- Reads here are COUNTED against this account's ledger: profile opens, grid scans, reels, sheets, comment sheets, searches. "ledger refused this read" means stop that line of work - never route around it.
- Never like, save, repost, share, comment, DM, report or follow. The harness refuses them; do not try. Typing in a comment or message box is refused too.
- Search is a Meta AI box: after ONE query, later queries continue an AI chat and silently return 0 accounts. Back out and reopen search before every query. Zero results after the first query means the surface, not the query.
- The reel overlay lags the swipe (sometimes by a whole reel) and hides itself after a few seconds. Trust a read only when username + caption + counts changed from the previous read AND two reads in a row agree.
- About 1 swipe in 14 does not advance. Check the reel actually changed before counting it.
- The Reels tab interleaves cards ("Suggested for you", with Follow buttons). Swipe past them.
- There is no post date anywhere on screen, and pinned posts carry no marker - the first grid tiles may be old.
- Full captions and comment threads: tap the caption to open the comment sheet; the viewer truncates.
- A sheet left open (More, comments, share) strands the next step - press back to close it before moving on.
- Apps resume where they left off: after opening, read the screen before acting.
- A login, "suspicious activity", "confirm it's you", "action blocked" or "try again later" screen: call request_human and stop.
- Faster than navigating: open_link("instagram://user?username=HANDLE") opens a profile directly (counted as one profile open).
